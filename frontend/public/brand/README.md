# Brand asset drop-in slots

The app ships with crisp **SVG** brand marks (favicon, d20, wordmark) and needs
no raster art to run. Drop final photographic assets here to light up the richer
moments (referenced by the landing hero, share cards, and social previews):

| File | Purpose | Recommended size |
|------|---------|------------------|
| `logo.png` | Full dragon hero logo (transparent) | 1200×630 |
| `mascot-dicewyrm.png` | Dicewyrm mascot illustration (transparent) | 512×512 |
| `header-banner.jpg` | Marketing header banner | 1600×400 |
| `og-image.png` | Social / OpenGraph preview (referenced in index.html) | 1200×630 |

All art should follow `docs/branding.md` (Dragon Red / Ancient Gold on Deep
Slate). These files are intentionally not committed — add your own.
