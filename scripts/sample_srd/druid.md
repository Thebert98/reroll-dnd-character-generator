# Druid

Druids wield the powers of nature and adopt animal forms. They revere nature
above all, gaining their spells and other magical powers from the force of
nature itself or from a nature deity.

## Class Features: Hit Points
**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per druid level.
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier.
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per
druid level after 1st.

## Class Features: Proficiencies
**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields (druids will not wear armor or use
shields made of metal).
**Weapons:** Clubs, daggers, darts, javelins, maces, quarterstaffs, scimitars,
sickles, slings, spears.
**Saving Throws:** Intelligence, Wisdom.

## Spellcasting
Druids are full spellcasters. A druid prepares spells from the druid spell list.
Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your druid spells. You can cast a druid
spell as a ritual if that spell has the ritual tag and you have the spell
prepared. Druids do not have access to the wizard or sorcerer spell lists; for
example, a druid cannot learn Fireball.

## Wild Shape
Starting at 2nd level, you can use your action to magically assume the shape of a
beast that you have seen before. You can use this feature twice, regaining
expended uses on a short or long rest.
